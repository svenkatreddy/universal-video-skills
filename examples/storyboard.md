# Example: Storyboard (text version)

Project: "Night Market" (45-second short, 8 shots).
Style lens: 1970s paranoia thriller: 35mm, natural light, muted warm
grade, subtle grain.

Each panel: shot #, camera, story job, duration, generation strategy.

---

**Panel 1:** Wide, slow crane down. Establish the market at blue hour.
8s. Text-to-video. (Quiet panel: let the rain and neon do the work.)

**Panel 2:** Medium tracking shot, waist height, L→R. Mara crosses the
market, hunting for someone. 8s. Text-to-video, asset mara-01.
See `shot-brief.md` for the full brief.

**Panel 3:** Push in on the red umbrella. She spots it: he's here. 6s.
Image-to-video (lock the umbrella's exact red first).

**Panel 4:** Handheld-feel tracking, L→R. She follows through the crowd.
8s. Text-to-video. Screen direction must continue L→R.

**Panel 5:** Locked-off wide. The empty corner. He's gone. 5s.
Text-to-video. (Quiet panel: the beat of absence.)

**Panel 6:** Low-angle close-up. The umbrella lies abandoned in the alley,
rain hammering it. 6s. Image-to-video (umbrella asset).

**Panel 7:** Medium close-up, slow push in. Mara laughs at herself, then
notices the dumpling-cart guy watching her. 8s. Image-to-video with locked
first frame (performance shot; asset mara-01).

**Panel 8:** Overhead, slow drift. Two bowls, steam rising. She sits down:
"One bowl." 8s. Text-to-video. Warm ending; dialogue timed 4–6s.

---

Join notes: cut on motion where possible (2→3 on her turn, 4→5 on the
corner). Fade through black into panel 8 (time passing, mood shift).
Screen direction: L→R throughout panels 2–4; panel 7 is frontal (neutral).
